Our Foundation

From Traditional PM to AI-Powered Consultant

The Challenge

KEDAL's founder is a PMP-certified leader who has driven operational transformation across healthcare, technology, government, and professional services. She's partnered with C-suite executives, managed cross-functional teams, and delivered results in every sector KEDAL serves. That breadth of experience is the foundation of our consulting approach. As the landscape evolved, she recognized the opportunity to amplify that expertise with AI — authentically, without hype, and in a way that could be taught to others.

The Approach

  • Built a structured learning roadmap — phased and milestone-driven, the same methodology we now use with clients
  • Tested and evaluated multiple AI platforms, matching each tool to its strengths rather than defaulting to one vendor
  • Applied intelligent automation to real consulting workflows through deliberate, documented practice
  • Turned the entire journey into a repeatable framework that clients can follow

The Results
