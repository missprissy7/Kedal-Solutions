Our Story

Kedal Solutions was founded on a straightforward belief: organizations deserve consulting partners who bring real solutions, not just recommendations. We started in healthcare one of the most operationally complex and highly regulated industries there is and that foundation shaped everything about how we work today.

At our core, we are human-centered consultants. We listen first, understand the people behind the process, and design solutions that support teams instead of overwhelming them.

We help organizations streamline operations, strengthen their teams, and make better decisions. Intelligent tools and AI enhance how we do that; giving us the speed and depth to deliver stronger results. And we help our clients integrate those same capabilities into their own operations, so improvements keep compounding long after the engagement ends.
